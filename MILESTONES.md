## Deadline policy

- A milestone may slip once by no more than three calendar days.
- A second miss triggers scope reduction, not a longer schedule.
- The production date cannot move until excluded features and optional work have been cut again.
- Friday is integration and completion day. No new features begin Friday.
- Each milestone ends with evidence, a retrospective of no more than ten minutes, and a narrowed next milestone.

## Work-in-progress policy

- Maximum three active outcomes across all AIBRY work.
- Maximum one active AIBRY Command milestone.
- Maximum one `In Progress` action per owner.
- Starting a fourth outcome requires freezing another.
- A task larger than one working day must be split.
- A feature unfinished after two sessions must be reduced to its smallest usable form.

## Idea quarantine

- Capture new ideas in `PARKING_LOT.md`.
- Set review eligibility at least 72 hours after capture.
- Do not add ideas to the active sprint outside the weekly CEO review.
- Emergency exceptions must protect security, data, production availability, or a hard external deadline.
- Excitement is not an emergency.

## Completion Mode

Completion Mode lasts 72 hours and allows only:

- Finishing current work
- Bug fixes
- Tests
- Documentation
- Data cleanup
- Deployment and verification

It forbids new features, repositories, branding passes, architecture changes, and speculative refactors.

Completion Mode starts when:

- Two milestones are partially complete simultaneously
- The same feature remains unfinished after two work sessions
- Scope expands twice within seven days
- Verified output does not increase for three days despite heavy activity

## High-energy safeguard

High energy may accelerate approved work. It cannot expand scope, spending, permissions, public promises, launch dates, or external commitments.

These actions require a 24-hour cooling period and daylight review:

- Purchases or new recurring services
- Public announcements
- Destructive migrations or deletion
- Production credential, identity, DNS, or access changes
- Partnerships, client commitments, or launch promises
- Freezing or abandoning the flagship

If sleep is substantially disrupted or judgment feels unusually accelerated, apply a 48-hour hold and seek a trusted person's review for irreversible decisions. This is an operating safeguard, not a punishment.

## Momentum recovery

If meaningful progress stops for three days:

1. Stop adding active work.
2. Run a 15-minute status audit.
3. Find the smallest deployable vertical slice.
4. Freeze or delete optional unfinished work.
5. Begin one task capped at 45 minutes.
6. Enter Completion Mode for 72 hours.
7. Do not restart the project, redesign it, or create a cleaner replacement repository.

Ask: **What is the smallest action that restores visible forward motion today?**
