# AIBRY Command - Parking Lot

This file preserves ideas without allowing them to hijack the active sprint.

## Rules

- Capture ideas immediately but do not implement them.
- Every idea receives a capture timestamp and a review date at least 72 hours later.
- Promotion can occur only during the weekly CEO review.
- Promoting an MVP feature requires removing one of equal or greater effort.
- Rejected ideas remain recorded with a short reason.
- Emergency promotion is limited to security, data loss, production availability, or a hard external deadline.
- Excitement, novelty, and `while we are here` are not emergencies.
